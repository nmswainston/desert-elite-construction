Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to desertelitehomesandconstruction.com, operated by Desert Elite Homes & Construction (AZ ROC 353956), a residential contractor serving Scottsdale, Cave Creek, Carefree, Phoenix, and the surrounding area.

We keep this short and specific on purpose. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anything other than what is described below.

Information you give us

If you submit the quote request form, we collect what you type into it:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • City
  • The service you are interested in
  • Your project timeline
  • Whatever you write in the message field

We use this only to respond to your enquiry and to discuss the work you asked about. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this one records some technical information when you visit:

  • Which pages you viewed and how long you stayed
  • The website or search that sent you here
  • Your general location, at the city or region level, estimated from your IP address
  • Your device type, browser, and screen size

This is aggregate information about how the site is used. It is not used to identify you personally, and we do not attempt to link it to a named individual.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages are useful to them. Google Analytics sets cookies, which are small files stored in your browser, to recognise a repeat visit from the same device.

You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on. The site works normally either way. We do not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, because there is no agreed standard for what that response should be.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not run retargeting or remarketing.

Services we rely on

Running this site involves a few third parties. Each one receives only what it needs to do its job:

  • Netlify hosts the website and processes form submissions. Their servers keep standard access logs.
  • Google Analytics provides the visitor statistics described above.
  • Google Fonts and Cloudflare deliver the fonts and icons the pages are built with. Loading any file from another server necessarily reveals your IP address to it, which is how the web works rather than a choice we made about your data.

Each of these companies handles data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep things

Quote requests are kept as business records for as long as they may be relevant to a current or prospective project, and in line with normal record-keeping for construction work. Analytics data is retained on a rolling basis under the settings of the analytics account. If you would like your enquiry deleted sooner, just ask.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will handle it. There is no charge, and you do not need to give a reason.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection, so what you submit through the form is protected in transit. No website can promise perfect security, but we do not store payment details or sensitive personal records on this site at all, which keeps the risk low.

Children

This site is intended for adults arranging construction work. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information through this site, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes to how we handle information will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or requests about your information, can be sent through our contact page.
